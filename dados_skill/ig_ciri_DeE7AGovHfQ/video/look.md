## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size close-up 48%, medium close-up 33%, medium shot 20%; subject is a face (shot size from face height)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 100%; typical move push-in
- Lighting: three-quarter key + fill (standard set-up). Key light frame left, three-quarter front, at subject height; lighting ratio about 1.5:1 (0.6 stops); soft light (wide lit-to-shadow ramp); colour temperature neutral (about 5000K); background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); normal saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall neutral
- Editing: 65.2 cuts per minute, 0.36s average live-action shot; 5 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 0 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 75%; 60% of cuts land in pauses of the speech (cutting on breaths)
- Format: talking head (face as subject 67%, objects / hands as subject 33%, handheld 100%)

**Shooting plan for the remake**
