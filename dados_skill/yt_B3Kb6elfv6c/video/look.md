## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium shot 100%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 100%; typical move push-in
- Lighting: night / low-light ambient, no dedicated fill. Key light frame right, three-quarter front, from above (ceiling light / street lamp / high source); lighting ratio about 1.6:1 (0.7 stops); hard light (sharp shadow edge); colour temperature cool (daylight / cool-white LED); background sharp
- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); high saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall cool
- Editing: 71.2 cuts per minute, 0.88s average live-action shot; 1 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 0 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 98%
