## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium close-up 59%, medium shot 22%, extreme close-up 12%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 93%, fixed camera (tripod / mount / on the desk) 4%, handheld 2%; typical move pull-out
- Lighting: night / low-light ambient, no dedicated fill. Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), at subject height; lighting ratio about 1.2:1 (0.3 stops); soft light (wide lit-to-shadow ramp); colour temperature warm (about 3000–4000K); the light in the dark carries strong colour: neon signs or RGB mood lights; background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- (not live footage: the above describes the light in the picture, for generation prompts, not a shooting plan)
- Grade: vivid, high saturation; high saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall warm
- Editing: 58.4 cuts per minute, 1.01s average shot; no live-action shots
